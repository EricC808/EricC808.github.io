## Overview

*No Service Tonight* is a surreal 2D psychological platformer about escaping a distorted version of a late-night workplace and finding a route home. The game combines platforming, environmental puzzles, dialogue, and liminal environments with a retro Windows-inspired visual style.

## What We Built

- Four complete levels with different settings and puzzle ideas.
- Platforming and clue-based progression.
- More than 100 lines of dialogue.
- Hand-drawn character art, pixel-art environments, and animated player sprites.
- A story focused on burnout, loneliness, and disassociation.

## My Experience

Working on this project gave me more experience with collaborative game development, level design, puzzle progression, testing, and combining separate pieces of a game into one complete experience. The team focused on finishing a full playable story rather than only building a small prototype.
